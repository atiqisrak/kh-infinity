# Phone Parts HS Codes and Import Duty in Bangladesh: A Classification Guide for Importers

> **Short answer:** Phone parts HS codes decide your import duty in Bangladesh. Displays go under heading 8524, lithium-ion batteries under 8507.60, chargers under 8504.40, laptop parts under 8473.30, earbuds under 8518.30 and other phone parts under 8517.79. Each code carries its own CD, RD, SD, VAT, AT and AIT, so check rates in the current Bangladesh Customs Tariff.

Getting phone parts HS codes right is the cheapest cost control a Bangladesh electronics importer has. The HS (Harmonized System) code on your Bill of Entry decides which duty lines apply, which documents customs expects, and how much scrutiny the consignment gets at Chattogram or Dhaka airport. A box of iPhone screens, a carton of power banks and a pallet of MacBook keyboards can arrive on the same flight from Shenzhen and still clear under three different headings, each with a different tax burden. ## What Is an HS Code and Why Does It Decide Your Duty?

An **HS code** is the international product classification number from the World Customs Organization's Harmonized System, used by customs authorities to identify goods and apply tariffs. The first six digits are shared worldwide. Bangladesh adds further digits for its national tariff lines.

The HS is revised roughly every five years. The current edition, [HS 2022](https://www.wcoomd.org/en/topics/nomenclature/instrument-and-tools/hs-nomenclature-2022-edition.aspx), entered into force on 1 January 2022. For our sector, the most important change was a new heading, **8524**, which the WCO defines as [flat panel display modules](https://www.wcotradetools.org/en/harmonized-system/2022/en/168524), whether or not incorporating touch-sensitive screens. Before 2022, display modules were classified according to the device they were built for. Since HS 2022, they have their own heading. That matters for repair-part importers, because a phone screen, a tablet screen and many other display assemblies now share one classification logic.

In Bangladesh, the code sets the rates for every layer of duty. One wrong digit can change the supplementary duty that applies, and through compounding, the VAT and advance taxes too.

## How Does Bangladesh Import Duty Work? The TTI Stack Explained

**Total Tax Incidence (TTI)** is the combined burden of all duties and taxes collected at import on a Bangladesh tariff line, expressed as a share of the assessable value. It is not one rate. It is a stack:

- **CD (Customs Duty):** the base tariff on the assessable value.
- **RD (Regulatory Duty):** an additional levy applied to many tariff lines.
- **SD (Supplementary Duty):** applied to selected goods, and often the line that surprises importers most.
- **VAT (Value Added Tax):** charged on a base that includes the assessable value plus the duties above, which is why it compounds.
- **AT (Advance Tax):** a VAT-related advance collected at import stage on commercial imports.
- **AIT (Advance Income Tax):** collected at import and creditable against income tax for importers who file properly.

We do not publish rates here, because they change with each national budget and through SROs during the year. Use the official sources: the NBR [tariff schedule](https://nbr.gov.bd/taxtype/tariff-schedule/eng), organised by fiscal year and chapter, and the Bangladesh Customs [operative tariff](https://bangladeshcustoms.gov.bd/trade_info/operating_center), searchable by HS code or description. For a worked example of how the layers compound on a real CIF value, read [why your Bangladesh import quote was wrong before the ship left port](/blog/bangladesh-import-real-cost-tti).

## What Happens If Phone Parts Are Misclassified?

Misclassification is when goods are declared under an HS code that does not match their physical nature and function. In Bangladesh it causes three kinds of cost:

- **Reassessment and demand notices.** If customs reclassifies to a line with higher SD or CD, the importer pays the difference. Penalties can follow, and the consignment waits until it is settled.
- **Physical examination.** A mismatch between invoice description, HS code and contents is a common trigger for a full container or carton inspection. That adds days at port or airport. Our post on [why Bangladesh import clearance gets delayed](/blog/why-bangladesh-import-clearance-delayed) walks through where these delays happen.
- **Overpayment you never recover.** Declaring under a higher-duty code "to be safe" is still a misclassification. It quietly cuts margin on every shipment.

Mixed consignments carry the highest risk. A single invoice line reading "mobile accessories" that covers screens, batteries, chargers and earbuds gives customs no basis to classify, so expect the highest-duty interpretation. Invoice each product type on its own line, with its own HS code, quantity and unit value.

## Which Documents Do You Need to Import Phone Parts into Bangladesh?

Most clearance problems for electronics come from missing or inconsistent paperwork rather than the goods themselves. A clean electronics file should include:

1. **Commercial invoice** with a precise product description, HS code per line, unit price, Incoterm and currency.
2. **Packing list** that matches the invoice line by line, with carton numbers, net and gross weights.
3. **Bill of lading or air waybill.**
4. **Certificate of Origin (CoO)** from the exporter's chamber or authorised body.
5. **Import documentation and payment records** under Bangladesh Bank rules, matched to the invoice value.
6. **For lithium-ion batteries:** a **UN38.3 test summary** and an **MSDS/SDS**. Airlines and forwarders will not accept lithium cargo without them. See our [phone battery import guide on UN38.3 and grades](/blog/phone-battery-import-bangladesh-un38-3-grades) and the companion post on [iPhone battery health diagnostics](/blog/iphone-battery-health-diagnostic-ti-batteries).
7. **For chargers and adapters:** supplier CE and RoHS documentation and clear output ratings on the invoice. Our [phone charger import guide on GaN and safety](/blog/phone-charger-import-bangladesh-gan-safety) explains what to check.
8. **For displays:** supplier spec sheets showing panel technology (LCD or OLED), which support the 8524 subheading you declare. Model-level sourcing is covered in our [Android display sourcing guide](/blog/android-display-sourcing-samsung-pixel-redmi-realme) and [iPad screen replacement parts guide](/blog/ipad-screen-replacement-parts-guide).

The [Bangladesh Trade Portal](https://www.bangladeshtradeportal.gov.bd/) is the government's official source of regulatory information for importers. Use it to check whether a product needs any additional permits before you place the order.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the HS code for mobile phone displays in Bangladesh?

Since HS 2022, phone display modules are classified under heading 8524, flat panel display modules. KHI declares LCD-based display assemblies under 8524.91.00. OLED modules fall under 8524.92, so match the subheading to the panel technology and keep the spec sheet on file.

### What is the HS code for phone batteries?

Lithium-ion phone batteries are classified under HS 8507.60, lithium-ion accumulators. They also need a UN38.3 test summary and an MSDS/SDS for air or sea freight. Without those documents, forwarders will usually refuse the cargo.

### How much import duty do I pay on phone parts in Bangladesh?

It depends on the HS code. Each tariff line carries its own combination of CD, RD, SD, VAT, AT and AIT, and rates change with the national budget. Check the current First Schedule through the NBR tariff schedule or the Bangladesh Customs operative tariff before you calculate landed cost.

### Can I declare all phone accessories under one HS code?

No. Screens, batteries, chargers, earbuds and laptop parts fall under different headings, and a single "mobile accessories" line invites reassessment and physical examination. Invoice and declare each product type separately with its own HS code.

### Why did the HS code for phone screens change in 2022?

HS 2022 introduced heading 8524 so that flat panel display modules are classified as products in their own right, rather than by the device they are built for. Importers who still declare screens under older parts headings risk a mismatch with the current tariff.

### What documents are needed to clear phone parts at Chattogram or Dhaka?

You need a commercial invoice, a packing list, a bill of lading or air waybill, and a Certificate of Origin, plus payment documentation under Bangladesh Bank rules. Batteries also need UN38.3 and MSDS, and chargers should carry supplier CE and RoHS documentation. Consistency between invoice, packing list and HS code matters as much as the documents themselves.
